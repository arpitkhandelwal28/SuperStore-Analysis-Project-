# 📊 SuperStore Sales Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** focused on analyzing SuperStore sales performance, profitability, customers, products, and regional trends to generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail business needs to understand which products, categories, customers, and regions are driving revenue and profit. The goal of this project is to transform raw SuperStore data into meaningful insights that can support better pricing, product, customer, and regional decisions.

## 📌 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products
- Compare sales and profitability across categories and regions
- Understand customer and segment performance
- Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identify trends and opportunities for business growth
- Present findings in a clear, decision-oriented format

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **SQL** — data querying and business analysis
- **Microsoft Excel** — data preparation and analysis
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboarding and visualization
- **DAX** — calculated measures and KPIs
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

The project focuses on business questions such as:

1. What are the key sales and profit KPIs?
2. Which categories and products generate the most revenue and profit?
3. Which regions and customer segments perform best?
4. Which products or segments require attention?
5. How do discounts affect sales and profitability?
6. What trends can be identified over time?
7. Where are the strongest opportunities for improving business performance?

## 📊 Key KPIs

The analysis can be used to track important retail metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Average Discount
- Profit Margin

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard is designed to provide an interactive view of sales and profitability performance, with business-focused visuals and filters for exploring categories, products, regions, and customer segments.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-performing categories and products that contribute strongly to revenue and profit
- Regions and customer segments with different levels of sales performance
- Products with weak profitability that may require pricing or discount optimization
- The relationship between discounting and profit margins
- Trends that can support better inventory, sales, and marketing decisions

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, a retail business can:

- Focus on high-profit products and categories
- Review products with consistently weak margins
- Optimize discount strategies instead of relying on heavy discounting
- Prioritize high-value customer segments and regions
- Use sales trends to improve inventory and promotional planning
- Monitor both revenue and profit rather than evaluating performance using sales alone
